Recipe Card

Paste a recipe URL to get it as a Cooking for Engineers-style card: ingredients down the left, steps as cells that merge the things they combine.

…or paste a recipe

Paste page HTML (View Source), schema.org JSON-LD, a Mealie recipe JSON export, or plain text with “Ingredients” and “Instructions” headings.

Fetch settings

Browsers can't read other sites directly, so pages are fetched through a CORS proxy. If one is down, try another, or run your own and enter its URL with {url} where the encoded recipe URL goes.

    Edit structure

    The steps are matched to ingredients automatically, and it won't always get it right. Each indented line feeds into the line above it. #3 is ingredient 3, and [2] links a cell to step 2's full text. Edit, then apply.
